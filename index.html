About

I’m Lauren, a computer science grad student at USC who likes building things people use and play with.

I like working across the whole stack: designing the API, building the interface someone actually clicks on, and writing the tests and pipelines that keep it all running. Lately that’s also meant machine learning, training vision models for textile recycling, and games, like the multiplayer shooter I built in Unreal.

I also teach. As a learning assistant for USC’s Video Game Programming course, I lead weekly tutorials and labs and give students detailed feedback on their code. Explaining a bug to someone else is the fastest way I know to understand it myself.
